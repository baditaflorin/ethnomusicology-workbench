# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and specific.
- Assume good intent while still naming problems clearly.
- Respect privacy, especially around field recordings and community materials.
- Prefer evidence and reproducible examples.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private recordings, credentials, or personal information.
- Repeated disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Concerns can be reported to baditaflorin@gmail.com. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other reasonable action to protect the project community.
